A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a still image of a lake at first light, a stone under the surface holding the whole sky. No imperatives, no address to the reader: one ripple crosses the water and settles, and the lake keeps the sky.

`main.py` (Python 3 stdlib only) writes the 246-code-point text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
